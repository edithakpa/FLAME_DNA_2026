
# 🔥 FLAME DNA

### Understanding Fire. Protecting Space.

**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — FLAMENOVA TECH**

FLAME DNA is an interactive scientific platform designed to explore microgravity combustion research, analyze NASA SAFFIRE experimental data, and support fire-safety research for future space missions.

## 📚 Scientific Context

Research based on NASA microgravity combustion studies, including SAFFIRE I, II and III.

FLAME DNA is an independent educational and research prototype developed for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. It is not an official NASA fire-safety system.
